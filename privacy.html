Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-29

01

Who we are

LabRef ("we," "our," "us") is a lab reference-values lookup application. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the LabRef app or this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

02

Information we collect

We aim to collect only what's needed to run LabRef:

  • Account information — your email address and password (or sign-in details) when you create an account.
  • Values you enter — lab test names and results you look up or save, so we can show you reference ranges and, if you're signed in, your saved history.
  • Basic device and usage data — general information like app version and crash logs, used only to fix bugs and improve reliability.

We do not ask for or knowingly collect government ID numbers, financial information, or your exact location.

03

How we use your information

  • To show you reference ranges and interpret the values you search for.
  • To let you save and revisit your test history if you're signed in.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal information.

04

How your information is stored

Your account details and saved test history are stored securely and are only accessible to you when signed in. We retain this information for as long as your account is active, or until you ask us to delete it.

05

Third-party services

LabRef may use third-party services for essential functions such as authentication, hosting, or analytics. Where this is the case, those providers only receive the minimum data needed to perform their function, and are bound by their own privacy and security practices.

06

Your choices and rights

  • You can view or edit your saved data from within the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below.
  • You can stop using LabRef and uninstall the app at any time.
07

Not medical advice

LabRef provides reference ranges and general information for educational purposes only. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Always consult a doctor or lab specialist about your specific results.

08

Children's privacy

LabRef is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as LabRef changes. If we make significant changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
